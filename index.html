Questions

Before you reserve a seat

Is a job in Dubai guaranteed?

No program can guarantee employment. Pillar Overseas provides training, certification, and structured access to employer interviews — the hiring decision is made by the employer.

Do I pay anything to join the waitlist?

No. Registering interest and the initial assessment call are free. Fees only apply once you're confirmed into a training cohort.

What if I'm not selected after training?

We're finalizing the specifics of this policy during Cohort 01 and will confirm it in writing before any fee is collected.

Is this cohort actually running yet?

Cohort 01 is in its formation stage. Reserving a seat now helps us confirm demand and plan the first intake.
