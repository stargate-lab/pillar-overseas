Hospitality & Housekeeping

Five-star housekeeping standards, guest service basics, and equipment and chemical safety — aligned to the hotel employers Pillar Overseas works with in Dubai.

Reserve This Track WhatsApp Us
Who this track is for

Guest-facing service, brought up to five-star standard

This track suits candidates interested in hospitality and guest-facing service roles — whether from prior housekeeping, hotel, or domestic service experience, or simply an interest in the field — who want it formalized into NVQ-aligned certification and a structured route toward employer interviews in the UAE.

You don't need prior hotel experience to apply. The assessment call after registration is where fit is discussed.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Indicative module breakdown for Cohort 01 — confirmed in full once your seat is reserved.

01

Five-Star Housekeeping Standards

The presentation and consistency standards five-star properties hold rooms to.

02

Guest Service & Communication

Guest-facing basics: courtesy, discretion, and handling requests professionally.

03

Equipment & Chemical Safety

Safe handling of cleaning equipment and chemicals used in hotel environments.

04

Room & Public Area Turnover

Turnover procedures and timing expectations for rooms and shared spaces.

05

Laundry & Linen Handling

Linen handling, laundry procedures, and fabric care basics.

06

Site-Ready Practical Assessment

A hands-on evaluation before you're introduced to employer partners.
